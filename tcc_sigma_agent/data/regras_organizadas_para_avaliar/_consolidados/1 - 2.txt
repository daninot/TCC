# Arquivo: A.yml
title: Bitbucket Unauthorized Full Data Export Triggered
id: e4f2a8c1-3b7d-4f9e-a5c2-1d6b8e3f2a74
status: stable
description: >
  Detects an unauthorized attempt to trigger a full data export on an Atlassian
  Bitbucket Data Center instance via the Data Pipeline feature. This event is
  emitted exclusively at the Advanced audit log level and indicates that a
  principal lacking the required system-admin privilege invoked the export
  endpoint. It is a high-confidence indicator of data exfiltration, credential
  compromise, or insider threat targeting the entire SCM repository corpus.
references:
  - https://confluence.atlassian.com/bitbucketserver/audit-log-events-776640423.html
  - https://confluence.atlassian.com/bitbucketserver/secret-scanning-1157471613.html
author: Senior Threat Detection Engineer
date: 2026-05-25
tags:
  - attack.exfiltration
  - attack.collection
  - attack.t1213.003
  - attack.t1530
logsource:
  product: bitbucket
  service: audit
detection:
  selection:
    action: 'Unauthorized full data export triggered'
  condition: selection
falsepositives:
  - Misconfigured automation or CI/CD service accounts attempting a scheduled
    export without the correct system-admin role assigned.
  - Penetration-test or red-team exercises with prior written approval.
level: high
