title: LSASS Process Access with Credential Dumping Access Rights
id: f1e3c8b2-4d7a-4f2e-b9c5-3a6d1e8f4b27
status: experimental
description: >
  Detects process access requests to the Local Security Authority Subsystem
  Service (lsass.exe) with GrantedAccess flags consistent with credential
  dumping tooling in the Mimikatz family. LSASS holds NTLM hashes, Kerberos
  tickets, and cleartext credentials for interactively logged-on users.
  Access mask families such as 0x1F0FFF and its variants request full process
  access including VM_READ and PROCESS_DUP_HANDLE, which are the minimum
  rights needed to extract credential material from LSASS memory. The 0x1010
  and 0x1410/0x1438 families represent PROCESS_QUERY_LIMITED_INFORMATION
  combined with VM_READ, the minimum access set used by modern dumpers
  attempting to reduce their footprint. Detection combines mandatory targeting
  of lsass.exe with a broad catalog of suspicious access mask values, then
  aggressively suppresses known-legitimate sources including Windows Defender,
  common EDR products, virtualisation agents, and developer tooling to reduce
  false-positive volume in instrumented environments.
