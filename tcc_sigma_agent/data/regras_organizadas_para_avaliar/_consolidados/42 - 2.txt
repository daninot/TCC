# Arquivo: E.yml
title: Okta Policy Rule Modification or Deletion
id: 3b6f9a2e-8c4d-4f7a-9b1e-2d3c4a5b6e7f
status: experimental
description: Detects modifications or deletions of Okta policy rules. Adversaries with administrative access may weaken or remove security policies (MFA enforcement, sign-on rules, access controls) to facilitate persistent access or privilege escalation. Requires Okta System Log ingestion and monitoring of eventType values for policy rule update and delete operations.
author: Senior Threat Detection Engineer
date: 2026/06/12
references:
  - [https://developer.okta.com/docs/reference/api/system-log/](https://developer.okta.com/docs/reference/api/system-log/)
  - [https://developer.okta.com/docs/reference/api/event-types/](https://developer.okta.com/docs/reference/api/event-types/)
tags:
  - identity
  - okta
  - tampering
  - privilege_escalation
logsource:
  product: okta
  service: okta
  category: identity_management
  description: Okta System Log events (system.log) containing eventType, actor, target, and outcome fields.
detection:
  selection_event:
    eventType|in:
      - 'policy.rule.update'
      - 'policy.rule.delete'
      - 'policy.rule.lifecycle.update'
      - 'policy.rule.lifecycle.delete'
  condition: selection_event
  fields:
    - eventType
    - actor.displayName
    - actor.alternateId
    - target.displayName
    - target.id
    - outcome.result
falsepositives:
  - Legitimate administrative changes by authorized identity or security teams during policy updates or maintenance windows.
  - Automated policy management or orchestration tools performing expected rule rotations or cleanups.
level: high
